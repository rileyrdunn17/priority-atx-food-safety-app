# priority-atx-food-safety-app
My app helps Austin restaurant goers decide which restaurants are the cleanest and worth visiting by evaluating existing restaurant inspection grades and health ratings.
> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Austin restaurant goers do not always realize the cleanliness and reality of the places they go to eat. My app helps them make a more informed decision and allows customers to feel confident in the quality and cleanliness of the restaurants they frequent.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Zip Codes:** How are the scores distributed and which zip codes score the lowest and are most frequently inspected?
2. **Monthly Performance:** Which month had the best performance?
3. **Distance:** Does distance from downtown (ie 600 Congress Ave 78701) correlate with score?

## Data
The [Food Establishment Inspection Scores]([https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset](https://data.austintexas.gov/stories/s/Food-Establishment-Inspection-Scores/7scf-8i7v)) is found on data.austintexas.gov, or the official data portal for the city of Austin. This dataset provides information about Food Inspection Scores reported by APH Environmental Health Officers.  Facilities in Austin receive 1-3 routine inspections per year depending on their overall risk score. The dataset is used for this project filters . This repo uses `food_establishment_inspection_scores.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: inspections that occured from July 1, 2026 to September 24, 2026.
